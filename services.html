Common Questions

Service FAQs

What equipment does the therapist bring? +
Your therapist arrives fully equipped with a professional massage table, fresh linens, pillows, massage oil or lotion, and any additional tools specific to your chosen service (hot stones, aromatherapy diffuser, etc.). You don't need to provide anything.
How much space is needed for a session? +
A massage table requires approximately 7ft x 3ft (210cm x 90cm) of clear floor space, plus a bit of room around it for the therapist to move. Most living rooms, bedrooms, and hotel rooms easily accommodate this.
Can I request a specific therapist? +
Absolutely. During booking, you can browse therapist profiles, review their specialties and ratings, and select your preferred therapist. If they're available for your chosen time slot, they will be confirmed for your session.
What if I'm not satisfied with my session? +
We stand behind every session with our 100% Satisfaction Guarantee. If you're not happy with your experience, contact us within 24 hours and we'll either send a different therapist for a complimentary session or issue a full refund — your choice.
How far in advance should I book? +
Same-day bookings are available! We recommend booking 24–48 hours in advance to ensure your preferred therapist and time slot, especially for weekend evenings. Peak times do fill up quickly.
