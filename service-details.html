The Experience

What to Expect

1

Arrival & Setup (15 min)

Your therapist arrives 15 minutes early to set up the professional massage table, fresh linens, and any requested add-ons. They'll discuss your areas of focus and pressure preferences.

2

The Massage

Your therapist works through 5 signature Swedish techniques: effleurage (gliding), petrissage (kneading), tapotement (tapping), friction, and vibration. Each movement flows into the next in a choreographed sequence.

3

Integration & Debrief

After the massage, your therapist allows you 5–10 minutes to rest and integrate. They'll share personalized aftercare recommendations before packing up and leaving your space pristine.

Benefits & Enhancements

Science-Backed Benefits

Deep Stress Relief

31% cortisol reduction after a single session, measurably lowering anxiety and mental tension.

Dramatically Better Sleep

Serotonin produced during Swedish massage converts to melatonin, improving deep sleep quality for up to 7 days.

Improved Circulation

Effleurage strokes mechanically stimulate blood flow, delivering oxygen and nutrients to tired muscles.

Muscle Recovery

Gentle kneading reduces lactic acid build-up and releases minor adhesions, accelerating muscle recovery.
