Common Questions

Therapist FAQs

Are all therapists fully licensed? +
Yes, every therapist on Serenova holds a valid state massage therapy license and is in full compliance with local regulations. We verify all licenses and re-verify them annually.
Can I request the same therapist every time? +
Absolutely — you can add therapists to your "Favourites" and always see their real-time availability. Regular clients often have standing weekly bookings with their preferred therapist.
What happens if my therapist cancels? +
In the rare event a therapist needs to cancel, you'll be notified immediately and we'll offer you a replacement therapist or reschedule at your convenience. Therapist cancellations incur no charge to you.
