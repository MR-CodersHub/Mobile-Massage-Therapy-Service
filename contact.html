Quick Answers

Contact FAQs

How quickly do you respond to messages? +
Our support team responds to all messages within 2 hours during business hours (7 AM – 10 PM PST, 7 days a week). For urgent booking issues, calling us directly is the fastest option.
Can I book over the phone? +
Yes! Call +1 (415) 555-0123 and our team will assist you with booking. However, our online booking system offers the most flexibility — including real-time therapist availability, instant confirmation, and full booking history.
I'm interested in a corporate wellness program. What are the next steps? +
Select "Corporate" in the inquiry type above and fill out the form with your company details and requirements. Our corporate team will reach out within 24 hours with a tailored proposal. Corporate programs start from $500/month for teams of 10+.
