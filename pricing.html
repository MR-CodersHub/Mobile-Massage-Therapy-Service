Pricing Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Are there any hidden fees? +
Never. All prices shown are complete and final. Travel within 15 miles is always included. If you're beyond 15 miles, a transparent mileage fee ($1.50/mile) is shown before you confirm your booking — no surprises at checkout.
Can I cancel or change my membership? +
Yes. Monthly memberships can be cancelled anytime with no penalty — just cancel before your next billing date. Annual plans can be paused for up to 3 months per year due to travel, pregnancy, or medical reasons.
What payment methods do you accept? +
We accept all major credit and debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, Amex, Discover), Apple Pay, Google Pay, and PayPal. All transactions are secured with 256-bit SSL encryption and PCI DSS compliance.
What's the cancellation policy for individual sessions? +
Cancel more than 2 hours before your session for a full refund. Late cancellations (under 2 hours) are charged 50% of the session fee. No-shows are charged the full session fee. Members can reschedule penalty-free up to 1 hour before.
