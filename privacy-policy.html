Serenova Privacy Policy

Serenova Mobile Wellness Inc. ("Serenova", "we", "us") provides on-demand mobile massage therapy at your home, hotel, or office. This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we collect when you use our website, booking platform, and mobile services, why we collect it, and the choices you have.

By creating an account, making a booking, or contacting us, you agree to the practices described here. If you do not agree, please do not use our services. For service rules, see our Terms & Conditions.

1

Introduction & Scope

This policy applies to all Serenova websites (including serenova.com), booking flows, client and therapist dashboards, newsletters, and customer support interactions. It covers both clients who receive massages and therapists who provide them.

We are committed to applicable privacy laws, including the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA), and where relevant the principles of the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and HIPAA-aligned safeguards for health-related notes you voluntarily share.

Our promise: we never sell your personal information. We share data only with the service providers needed to run your booking (payment processing, scheduling, support) and as required by law.
2

We collect information you provide directly, information generated when you use our services, and limited information from third parties.

3

How We Use Your Data

We use personal information only for legitimate, disclosed purposes:

  • Deliver services — confirming bookings, dispatching therapists, sending arrival and aftercare messages.
  • Personalize care — remembering pressure preferences, allergies, and favorite therapists (with your consent for health notes).
  • Payments & fraud prevention — charging for sessions, issuing refunds, detecting misuse.
  • Support — responding to questions, resolving disputes, and ensuring satisfaction-guarantee claims.
  • Improvement & marketing — aggregated analytics, and (only if you opt in) wellness tips, offers, and newsletters. You can unsubscribe at any time.

We do not use health notes for advertising, and we do not make automated decisions that produce legal effects about you.

4

Sharing & Disclosure

We share the minimum necessary data with:

  • Your assigned therapist — name, session location, service details, and any health notes you provided for that booking.
  • Service providers — secure payment processors, hosting, analytics, email/SMS, and customer-support tools bound by confidentiality and data-processing agreements.
  • Legal & safety — when required by law, to enforce our Terms, or to protect the safety of clients, therapists, or the public.
  • Business transfers — if Serenova is acquired or merged, with notice and continued protection under this policy.
What we never do: sell personal data, share health notes with advertisers, or disclose session locations publicly.
5

Cookies & Tracking

We use strictly necessary cookies for login, booking steps, theme (light/dark), and language/direction preferences; functional cookies to remember your choices; and, with consent, analytics cookies to understand aggregate usage.

  • Manage preferences anytime via your browser settings or the cookie banner.
  • Marketing emails always include an unsubscribe link; transactional booking messages are required to deliver your session.
  • We honor "Do Not Track" signals where technically feasible and do not use cross-site tracking for behavioral advertising.
6

Data Security

Security is built into every session — online and in person:

  • 256-bit SSL/TLS encryption in transit; encrypted storage at rest with least-privilege access.
  • Payments handled by PCI DSS-compliant processors; Serenova never stores full card numbers or CVVs.
  • HIPAA-aware handling of health notes: access limited to your therapist and essential support staff.
  • All therapists are license-verified, insured, and background-checked; sessions carry full liability coverage.
  • Incident response plan with prompt notification if a breach affects your data, as required by law.

No system is 100% secure. Please use a strong, unique password and contact us immediately if you suspect unauthorized access to your account.

7

Your Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or export your data; to opt out of marketing; to limit sensitive-data use; and to appeal a decision. California residents have CCPA/CPRA rights to know, delete, correct, and opt out of sale/sharing (we do not sell data).

  • Access & portability: request a copy of your bookings, profile, and preferences.
  • Correction: update your name, contact details, or health notes in your Client Dashboard or by email.
  • Deletion: request account deletion; we retain only what the law or legitimate operations require (e.g., tax receipts).
  • Marketing opt-out: one click in any email footer, or in dashboard notification settings.
  • Non-discrimination: exercising your rights will never affect your pricing or service quality.

To exercise any right, email privacy@serenova.com from your account email. We verify identity and respond within 30 days (45 where extended by law).

8

Retention & Children's Privacy

Retention: booking records are kept for 7 years for tax, insurance, and legal purposes; marketing consents until withdrawn; support tickets for 3 years; anonymized analytics indefinitely. Health notes are retained only as long as needed for safe care and deleted on verified request unless law requires otherwise.

Children: Serenova services are for adults 18+. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13 (or 16 where applicable). Prenatal and family bookings must be made by a parent or legal guardian. If you believe a child has provided data, contact us for prompt deletion.

International transfers: data is processed primarily in the United States with appropriate safeguards (contractual clauses, encryption) when transferred elsewhere.

9

Changes to This Policy & Contact

We may update this policy as our services evolve. Material changes will be announced by email or a prominent site notice at least 14 days before taking effect. The "Last updated" date above always reflects the current version.
