Serenova Terms and Conditions

These Terms & Conditions ("Terms") form a binding agreement between you ("Client", "you") and Serenova Mobile Wellness Inc. ("Serenova", "we", "us"), governing your use of our website, booking platform, dashboards, and mobile massage services.

By creating an account or booking a session, you confirm you are at least 18 years old, accept these Terms, and accept our Privacy Policy. If booking on behalf of someone else (e.g., a couples session or gift), you confirm you have their consent.

1

Agreement & Eligibility

1.1 Who we are. Serenova operates a marketplace that connects independent, licensed massage therapists with clients for outcall sessions at homes, hotels, offices, and event venues across 25+ US cities.

1.2 Eligibility. You must be 18+ with legal capacity to contract. Accounts are personal and non-transferable — you are responsible for all activity under your login. Prenatal massage requires confirmation of a healthy pregnancy; minors may only be present, not treated, unless local law and a guardian's written consent allow.

1.3 Service areas & hours. Bookings are available Mon–Sun, 7:00 AM – 10:00 PM PST (online booking 24/7), subject to therapist availability in your area. See service areas.

2

Services & Booking

2.1 What you book. Swedish, Deep Tissue, Aromatherapy, Sports, Prenatal, Couples, Hot Stone, and Reflexology sessions of 60, 90, or 120 minutes, plus add-ons (see Services and Pricing). Therapists bring a professional table, linens, oils, and music — you provide a clean, private space of roughly 6×10 ft.

  • Booking is confirmed only after payment authorization and an on-platform confirmation with therapist name, photo, and ETA.
  • Requests for a specific therapist are honored when possible but not guaranteed; a vetted substitute of equal qualification may be assigned.
  • Couples massage requires space for two tables and is staffed by two therapists.
  • Hotel/Airbnb bookings: you must ensure building access and comply with venue guest policies.

2.2 Accurate information. You agree to provide a correct address, access instructions, and honest health disclosures (injuries, conditions, pregnancy, allergies). Therapists may adapt or decline techniques for your safety.

4

Cancellation, Rescheduling & Refunds

Life happens — our policy is designed to be fair to both clients and therapists:

  • Free cancellation/reschedule up to 2 hours before your session start, via dashboard, confirmation link, or by phone.
  • Late cancellation (under 2 hours) or no-show: 100% of the session fee applies, as your therapist has reserved the time and traveled.
  • Therapist cancellation: if we must reassign or cancel, you are never charged — and we will prioritize finding a replacement or issue a full refund / credit.
  • Satisfaction guarantee: tell us within 24 hours if something was wrong and we will make it right — re-book, partial credit, or refund at our reasonable discretion.
Tip: running late? Message your therapist from your confirmation page. Sessions shortened by late client arrival are charged in full but therapists will always deliver the remaining time.
5

Client Responsibilities & Safety

To keep every session safe and professional, you agree to:

  • Provide a safe, hygienic, smoke-free, private space with access to hand-washing; secure pets in another room.
  • Ensure an adult (18+) is present for the entire session; ensure minors and non-participants give the therapist privacy.
  • Treat therapists with respect. Zero tolerance for harassment, sexual solicitation, discrimination, intoxication, or unsafe conditions — the therapist may end the session immediately with full charge and account review.
  • Disclose health issues honestly and follow therapist guidance on positioning, draping, and pressure. Draping is mandatory at all times.

Therapists are certified, licensed where required, background-checked, insured, and bound by a professional code of ethics. Only the top ~15% of applicants are accepted.

6

Health Disclaimer

Massage therapy supports relaxation and muscular relief but is not a substitute for medical diagnosis or treatment. We do not provide medical advice. Consult your physician before booking if you have (among others) deep-vein thrombosis, fractures, severe osteoporosis, contagious skin conditions, fever, recent surgery, high-risk pregnancy, or cancer under treatment.

Therapists may decline or modify treatment if contraindicated. If you feel discomfort, dizziness, or pain, tell your therapist immediately. In an emergency call 911 first, then notify us.
