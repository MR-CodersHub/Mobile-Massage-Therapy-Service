Our Story

Wellness That Comes to You

We started with one simple belief: premium wellness should never require a commute. Serenova brings spa-quality massage to your door — effortlessly.

2018 Founded
How We Began

Born from a Simple Frustration

In 2018, our founder Maya Chen was a busy entrepreneur in San Francisco. Despite earning well, she couldn't find time for the self-care she desperately needed. Every spa booking meant an hour of travel, rushed parking, and rigid appointment windows.

She thought: "Why can't the spa come to me?" Six months later, Serenova was born — a platform connecting certified therapists directly with clients in their own homes, hotels, and offices.

Today, with over 12,000 sessions completed across 25+ cities and a network of 340+ certified, background-checked therapists, Serenova is redefining what accessible luxury wellness means.

What Drives Us

Our Mission & Values

Our Mission

To make premium, therapeutic massage accessible to everyone — removing barriers of time, location, and inconvenience so that wellness becomes a seamless part of daily life.

Our Vision

A world where every person has easy access to expert touch therapy — where stress is managed proactively and physical well-being is not a luxury, but a universal right.

Authenticity

Real therapists. Real care. No shortcuts — every session is delivered with genuine expertise and personal attention.

Safety First

Comprehensive background checks, license verification, and insurance for every therapist on our platform.

Excellence

We only accept the top 15% of applicants. Continuous education and client feedback keep our standards impeccably high.

Compassion

We treat each client as a whole person — listening, adapting, and always putting your comfort and well-being first.
